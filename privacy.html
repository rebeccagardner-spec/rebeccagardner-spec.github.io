Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026  |  Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LGMediaStudioApps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the HomeCatalog app (“HomeCatalog” or the “app”). HomeCatalog is a personal home-inventory tool for documenting your belongings, receipts, and warranties.

The short version: HomeCatalog has no accounts, and everything you put in it stays on your device. We don’t collect your inventory, we can’t see it, and we don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

1. Information stored on your device

When you use HomeCatalog, the information you enter is saved only on your device. This includes:

  • The name you choose to be called in the app, if you enter one.
  • Home names, addresses, and photos.
  • Rooms, categories, collections, and item details, such as names, descriptions, brands, model or serial numbers, purchase dates, purchase prices, estimated values, condition notes, and warranty dates.
  • Photos and documents you add, such as item photos, receipts, warranties, and certificates.
  • Reports, reminders, and app settings.

This information isn’t uploaded to HomeCatalog servers, and we have no access to it. You don’t need to create an account or give us an email address, phone number, or password to use HomeCatalog.

2. Information we receive

HomeCatalog doesn’t send us any information from the app. It doesn’t include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it doesn’t collect your location, contacts, or browsing activity. HomeCatalog doesn’t use artificial intelligence to analyze your photos or documents.

The only information we receive is what you choose to send us. If you email LGMediaStudioApps@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it for up to 12 months, unless we need it longer to resolve an issue or meet a legal requirement.

3. Purchases

HomeCatalog Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or Apple’s App Store on iOS). Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their privacy policies. The app only checks with Google Play or the App Store whether Premium has been purchased, so it can unlock Premium and restore it on a new device. We don’t receive your payment card number or billing details.

4. Backups you create

Because your catalog lives only on your device, HomeCatalog lets you save a backup file and reminds you to do it regularly. You decide where each backup goes: for example, your own Google Drive, an email to yourself, or your computer. When you save a backup to another service, that service stores the file under its own privacy policy. We never receive your backup files.

Backup files aren’t password-protected, so store them somewhere private. When you restore a backup, its contents are loaded onto your device.

Android device backup: HomeCatalog is excluded from Android’s automatic device backup, so your catalog isn’t copied to your Google account. Use HomeCatalog’s own backup to keep a copy or move to a new phone.

5. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo of an item, receipt, or document.
  • Photos and files: used only to attach the specific images or files you select, or to open a backup file you choose to restore.
  • Notifications: used only to show reminders you set up, including backup reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: used only if you turn it on for App Lock.

You can turn off any permission in your device settings. HomeCatalog will keep working, but features that need that permission won’t be available.

6. App Lock

You can protect HomeCatalog with a PIN and, if you choose, your device’s fingerprint or face unlock. Your PIN is never stored as typed. Only a protected, one-way version of it is saved on your device. Fingerprint and face recognition are handled by your device’s operating system, and HomeCatalog never receives your fingerprint or face data.

Because nothing is stored outside your device, we can’t reset a forgotten PIN. If you forget it and haven’t turned on fingerprint or face unlock, the only option is to erase HomeCatalog’s data on your device and restore from a backup if you have one.

7. Security

Your catalog is protected by your device’s own security, such as its screen lock and storage encryption, and by App Lock if you turn it on. Because we don’t hold your data, a breach of our systems can’t expose your inventory. Keep your device’s software up to date and keep your backups somewhere private.

8. Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any home, item, photo, or document in the app at any time. To delete everything, uninstall HomeCatalog or choose Erase HomeCatalog on this device in the app’s Data Rights Center (also available from the App Lock screen). This permanently removes HomeCatalog’s data from your device. It doesn’t delete backup files you saved elsewhere, so delete those yourself if you want them gone.

HomeCatalog doesn’t have accounts, so there’s no account to delete and nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete support emails you sent us, email LGMediaStudioApps@gmail.com.

Deleting your data doesn’t refund a Premium purchase. Refunds are handled under Google Play’s or Apple’s refund policies.

9. Children’s privacy

HomeCatalog is intended for adults and isn’t directed to children. We don’t knowingly receive personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information we hold about you. Because your catalog stays on your device, you can already do all of this yourself in the app. For support emails we hold, contact us and we’ll respond within the time required by law. We don’t sell or share personal information for advertising.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HomeCatalog changes. We’ll update the “Last updated” date above, and if a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
